# What happens at your first visit?

Every place works a little differently. This is a general idea of what to
expect, so the first step feels less unknown.

## Before you go

- Call or check ahead if you can. Ask about opening hours and whether you need an appointment.
- You can bring someone you trust with you.
- If you take any medicines, bring a list of them or the packets.
- Write down any questions you want to ask, so you do not have to remember them.

## When you arrive

- Someone will welcome you and may ask you to fill in a short form.
- A staff member will talk with you about how things are going and what you would like help with.
- You can share as much or as little as you are ready to. It is fine to say "I am not ready to talk about that yet".
- You can ask who will see your information and how it is kept private.

## Questions you might ask

- What kinds of support do you offer?
- How often would I come, and for how long?
- Is there a cost?
- Can my family be involved, if I want them to be?

## Looking after yourself

- It is normal to feel nervous. Coming at all is a real step.
- If you are worried about how your body or mind will feel as you cut down or stop, tell the staff. They are the right people to advise you.
- If you feel unwell, or someone may have taken too much, get help now. The help button is on every screen.
