# About this app

Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD

Recovery Companion supports you between treatment sessions. It does **not**
replace treatment, counselling or medical care, and it does not give medical
advice.

If you are in danger, might hurt yourself, or think someone has overdosed,
contact emergency services now. Help contacts are on the Help screen.

This is a demo project. Please do not enter real personal information.
